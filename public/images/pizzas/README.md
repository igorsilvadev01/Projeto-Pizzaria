# Imagens das pizzas

As quatro imagens de apresentação foram criadas com a ferramenta integrada Imagegen. Os arquivos WebP preservam o fundo transparente. São imagens ilustrativas, e os ingredientes da receita continuam definidos em `lib/pizza-details.js`.

## margherita

Arquivo: `margherita.webp`

Prompt utilizado:

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: pizza restaurant website isolated hero product photograph. Create ONE whole round handmade Neapolitan pizza, Italian tomato sauce, melted fresh fior di latte mozzarella in irregular white pools, fresh bright green basil leaves, subtle olive oil sheen. No meat. Golden puffy crust with small natural char marks, extremely appetizing true food photography, artisanal not perfect geometry. Strict straight overhead camera, entire pizza visible with a clean margin around all sides, centered square composition. Soft warm daylight from upper left, rich real textures, no strong cast shadow. Genuine transparent background, no board, no plate, no hands, no accessories, no text. This asset is for SPADONI's cream and terracotta editorial pizza menu.
